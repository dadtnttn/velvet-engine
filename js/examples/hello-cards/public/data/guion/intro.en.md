## controls
**Lupe:** Drag one card onto another to stack them. If it does not fit, it goes back.
**Lupe:** Put a villager on a forest, a quarry or a berry bush: they gather without using anything up.
**Lupe:** Two pieces of wood and a villager make a plank. Two planks and a stone make a house!
**Lupe:** Space pauses time. The 1, 2 and 3 keys change the speed.
**Lupe:** F5 quick-saves, F9 quick-loads, and Escape opens the menu.
