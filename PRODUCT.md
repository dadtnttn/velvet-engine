# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Adult players using a keyboard-first visual-novel game. They need to understand the current choice, story state, and available actions quickly while staying immersed in Sam's nocturnal city.

## Product Purpose

Sam: Honest Stranger is an adult narrative game about Sam, a woman from a world without lies, navigating survival, work, intimacy, and social pressure in an unfamiliar city. The interface should make story choices easy to read while giving the game a distinct, memorable identity.

## Brand Personality

Punk, nocturnal, direct. The menu should feel urban and intimate, with a mature edge and visible character rather than generic visual-novel polish.

## Anti-references

Avoid childish anime presentation, glossy mobile-game UI, fantasy-game ornament, noisy neon overload, generic stock character poses, and dense menus that compete with the story.

## Design Principles

- Keep Sam the visual anchor of every major entry point.
- Make choices and navigation immediately legible.
- Use restrained urban tension: dark surfaces, deliberate pink accents, and controlled contrast.
- Let character expression and posture carry narrative information.
- Preserve a mature, grounded tone across scenes and interface assets.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep keyboard navigation visible and predictable, maintain strong text contrast, avoid color-only state changes, provide readable focus states, and respect reduced-motion preferences. No additional user-specific accommodations are currently known.
